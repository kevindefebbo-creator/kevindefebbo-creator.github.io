Kevin DeFebbo Career Timeline

Earlier local-government experience

Public records document municipal-management experience before DeFebbo's move to Kentucky, including service connected with Shippensburg, Pennsylvania and Schenectady County, New York. Earlier records also identify him in municipal government in Follansbee, West Virginia.

2005–2006: Bowling Green appointment

WKU Public Radio reported in November 2005 that Bowling Green's City Commission unanimously selected Kevin DeFebbo as city manager. He began the role in early 2006.

Bowling Green tenure

DeFebbo's tenure included responsibility for professional city administration and financial and organizational management. City financial reports from the period provide additional primary-source context.

2016: City Care Center

Reporting by The Lane Report documented the opening of the City Care Center for eligible Bowling Green employees and dependents, including comments from DeFebbo about healthcare quality, convenience and taxpayer savings.

Leadership development

WKU's alumni publication later documented successor Jeff Meisel describing DeFebbo as a mentor.
