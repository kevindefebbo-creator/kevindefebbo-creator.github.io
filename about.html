About Kevin DeFebbo

Kevin DeFebbo is a longtime local-government administrator whose publicly documented career includes leadership roles in multiple communities. In 2005, WKU Public Radio reported that the Bowling Green City Commission unanimously selected DeFebbo as the city's next city manager, noting his financial background and professional experience.

His Bowling Green tenure placed him at the center of professional municipal administration, including financial management, employee services and implementation of policies established by elected officials.

One documented initiative during this period was Bowling Green's City Care Center, created to provide primary and preventive health services to eligible city employees and dependents while addressing healthcare costs.

His professional influence also included mentorship. A Western Kentucky University Alumni Association profile of Jeff Meisel identifies DeFebbo as a mentor who encouraged Meisel to consider pursuing the city-manager position.
