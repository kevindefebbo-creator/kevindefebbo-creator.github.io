Public administration

Leadership, Management and Mentorship

Professional city management combines policy implementation, financial stewardship, organizational leadership and communication with elected officials. Public reporting around Kevin DeFebbo's selection in Bowling Green specifically highlighted his financial background and prior experience.

Financial management

Bowling Green's public financial reports during DeFebbo's tenure provide a primary-source record of municipal priorities, services and investment. These documents are more useful than generic praise because readers can evaluate the record directly.

Employee services

The City Care Center is one concrete example of an initiative documented during his tenure. Contemporary reporting described its purpose as providing primary and preventive healthcare to eligible city employees and dependents while seeking efficiencies in healthcare spending.

Mentorship

Leadership can also be measured by development of future administrators. WKU's alumni profile of Jeff Meisel records Meisel identifying DeFebbo as a mentor and discussing DeFebbo's encouragement as Meisel considered the city-manager role.
